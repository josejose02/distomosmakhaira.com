IotaIota Support

IotaIota: Learn Biblical Greek, for iPhone. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Contact us

Write to contacto@distomosmakhaira.com. We usually reply within two working days. If your question is about your subscription or your data, include the Support ID shown near the end of IotaIota, Settings.

Studying

How does IotaIota work?

IotaIota teaches the Greek of the New Testament the way seminaries do: vocabulary with spaced repetition, parsing of real verb forms from the New Testament, and paradigms. Each day, Today gives you the reviews that are due and new words from your current chapter. Course shows your chapters, Practice lets you drill parsing, paradigms and your weak spot, Community compares your week with your groups, and Progress shows your streak and accuracy.

Can I follow my textbook?

Yes. In IotaIota, Settings, Course, choose the chapter order of your textbook:

  • Basics of Biblical Greek (Mounce, 4th ed.), Track 1 or Track 2.
  • Fundamental Greek Grammar (Voelz).
  • Learn to Read New Testament Greek (Black).
  • A Primer of Biblical Greek (Croy).
  • Reading Koine Greek (Decker).
  • Beginning with New Testament Greek (Merkle and Plummer).
  • Or the Koine Path, our own order by frequency.

IotaIota follows which words and grammar each chapter introduces. The glosses and explanations are our own. If you change textbook, what you already learned stays: the words are the same. IotaIota is not affiliated with the publishers of these textbooks.

A gloss or a form looks wrong.

Write to us with the word or the form and the verse. The Greek text is the SBL Greek New Testament and the parsing comes from MorphGNT. See the content license page.

I don't get the daily reminder.
  • Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings, Notifications, IotaIota.
  • Check that the reminder is on in IotaIota, Settings, Reminder.
  • If you already reached your daily goal, IotaIota skips the reminder that day.
  • Focus modes can silence the reminder. Allow IotaIota in the Focus you use.
I changed phones. Where is my progress?

Your progress syncs through your private iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID and keep iCloud on for IotaIota (iOS Settings, your name, iCloud). It can take a few minutes to arrive.

Free plan and subscription

What is free?

Vocabulary with spaced repetition, Progress, Community, the reminder and app locking are free. Reviews that are due are never limited. New words per day on the free plan grow with your streak:

  • 0 to 2 days of streak: 5 new words a day.
  • 3 to 6 days: 7 words.
  • 7 to 13 days: 10 words.
  • 14 to 29 days: 15 words.
  • 30 days or more: 20 words.
What does IotaIota Premium add?

Parsing of real New Testament forms, paradigms (conjugations and declensions), the weak spot exercise, and as many new words a day as you choose in Settings.

How do subscriptions and free trials work?
  • There are two plans: monthly, with a 2 week free trial, and annual, with a 1 month free trial. The app shows the price in your currency before you subscribe.
  • Apple charges the subscription to your Apple ID when the trial ends and renews it automatically, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • IotaIota reminds you 2 days before your trial ends.
  • A free trial is available once per Apple ID.
How do I cancel?

iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, IotaIota, Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from IotaIota, Settings, Manage subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged.

I paid but IotaIota still shows the free plan.
  • Go to IotaIota, Settings, Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • Check your connection and open the app again.
  • If it still fails, write to us with your Support ID.
Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a code.

Go to IotaIota, Settings, Redeem a code.

Locking apps

How does app locking work?

In IotaIota, Settings, Lock apps, turn on "Lock apps until I review". Every day, from the time you choose (5:00 by default), your other apps lock until you review the number of words you chose (5, 10, 15 or 20). Then they unlock until the next day. Calls and emergencies always work. It is free.

Can I keep some apps unlocked?

Yes. In IotaIota, Settings, Lock apps, choose the "Apps that never lock".

The lock doesn't turn on.
  • IotaIota needs your Screen Time permission. Turn the option off and on again to be asked again.
  • Check that Screen Time is on in iOS Settings, Screen Time.
  • IotaIota sees only opaque identifiers of your apps: it never knows which apps you have or how much you use them.

Community

What do others see of me?

Only the members of your groups see you, and only after you create or join a group. By default you are anonymous: they see a fixed alias such as "Student 4821", your XP this week, your streak, your words mastered and your chapter. Never your answers. Your name appears only if you turn on Public profile in IotaIota, Settings, Community, Community privacy.

How do I report or block someone?

In a group's ranking, touch and hold a person (or swipe left on them) and choose Report or Block. To report a group, use the menu at the top of its ranking. When you block someone, you stop seeing them right away. We review reports within 24 hours and remove whatever breaks the rules. You can also write to us. To unblock, go to IotaIota, Settings, Community, Community privacy, Blocked profiles.

How do I delete my community data?

IotaIota, Settings, Community, Community privacy, Delete my community data. It deletes your profile, your memberships and the groups you created that have no other members. Your study progress is not touched.

Community says I need iCloud.

Community uses Apple's iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings to use it. Everything else in IotaIota works without it.

Privacy

What data does IotaIota collect?

See the privacy policy. In short: no accounts, your progress stays in your private iCloud, analytics are anonymous and ad measurement happens only with your consent.

Estudiar

¿Cómo funciona IotaIota?

IotaIota enseña el griego del Nuevo Testamento como en el seminario: vocabulario con repaso espaciado, análisis de formas verbales reales del Nuevo Testamento y paradigmas. Cada día, Hoy te da los repasos que tocan y palabras nuevas de tu capítulo actual. Curso enseña tus capítulos, Practicar te deja entrenar el análisis, los paradigmas y tu punto débil, Comunidad compara tu semana con la de tus grupos y Progreso enseña tu racha y tu precisión.

¿Puedo seguir mi libro de texto?

Sí. En IotaIota, Ajustes, Ruta, elige el orden de capítulos de tu libro:

  • Basics of Biblical Greek (Mounce, 4.ª ed.), Track 1 o Track 2.
  • Fundamental Greek Grammar (Voelz).
  • Learn to Read New Testament Greek (Black).
  • A Primer of Biblical Greek (Croy).
  • Reading Koine Greek (Decker).
  • Beginning with New Testament Greek (Merkle y Plummer).
  • O la Ruta Koiné, nuestro orden por frecuencia.

IotaIota sigue qué palabras y qué gramática introduce cada capítulo. Las glosas y las explicaciones son nuestras. Si cambias de libro, lo que ya aprendiste se queda: las palabras son las mismas. IotaIota no está afiliada a las editoriales de estos libros.

Una glosa o una forma parece incorrecta.

Escríbenos con la palabra o la forma y el versículo. El texto griego es el SBL Greek New Testament y el análisis viene de MorphGNT. Mira la página de licencia del contenido.

No me llega el recordatorio diario.

  • Comprueba que el recordatorio está activado en IotaIota, Ajustes, Recordatorio.

He cambiado de móvil. ¿Dónde está mi progreso?

Bloqueo de apps

¿Cómo funciona el bloqueo de apps?

En IotaIota, Ajustes, Bloquear apps, activa «Bloquear las apps hasta repasar». Cada día, desde la hora que elijas (las 5:00 si no la cambias), tus otras apps se bloquean hasta que repases las palabras que elegiste (5, 10, 15 o 20). Después se desbloquean hasta el día siguiente. Las llamadas y las emergencias siempre funcionan. Es gratis.

¿Puedo dejar algunas apps sin bloquear?

Sí. En IotaIota, Ajustes, Bloquear apps, elige las «Apps que nunca se bloquean».

El bloqueo no se activa.
  • IotaIota necesita tu permiso de Tiempo de uso. Desactiva la opción y vuelve a activarla para que te lo pida otra vez.
  • Comprueba que Tiempo de uso está activado en Ajustes de iOS, Tiempo de uso.
  • IotaIota solo ve identificadores opacos de tus apps: nunca sabe qué apps tienes ni cuánto las usas.

Comunidad

¿Qué ven los demás de mí?

Solo te ven los miembros de tus grupos, y solo después de que crees un grupo o te unas a uno. Por defecto eres anónimo: ven un alias fijo como «Estudiante 4821», tu XP de esta semana, tu racha, tus palabras dominadas y tu capítulo. Nunca tus respuestas. Tu nombre solo sale si activas el perfil público en IotaIota, Ajustes, Comunidad, Privacidad en Comunidad.

¿Cómo denuncio o bloqueo a alguien?

En la clasificación de un grupo, mantén pulsada a una persona (o desliza a la izquierda sobre ella) y elige Denunciar o Bloquear. Para denunciar un grupo, usa el menú de arriba de su clasificación. Al bloquear a alguien, dejas de verlo al momento. Revisamos las denuncias en menos de 24 horas y retiramos lo que incumpla las normas. También puedes escribirnos. Para desbloquear, ve a IotaIota, Ajustes, Comunidad, Privacidad en Comunidad, Perfiles bloqueados.

¿Cómo borro mis datos de la comunidad?

IotaIota, Ajustes, Comunidad, Privacidad en Comunidad, Borrar mis datos de la comunidad. Borra tu perfil, tus pertenencias y los grupos que creaste en los que no queda nadie más. Tu progreso de estudio no se toca.

La comunidad dice que necesito iCloud.

La comunidad usa iCloud de Apple. Inicia sesión en iCloud en los Ajustes de iOS para usarla. Todo lo demás de IotaIota funciona sin ella.

Privacidad

¿Qué datos recoge IotaIota?

Mira la política de privacidad. En pocas palabras: no hay cuentas, tu progreso se queda en tu iCloud privado, la analítica es anónima y la medición de anuncios solo ocurre con tu consentimiento.
